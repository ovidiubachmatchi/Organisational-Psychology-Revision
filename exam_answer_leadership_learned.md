Leadership - One thing I learned

One thing I found interesting was Heifetz idea of "getting on the balcony." It means that leaders need to step back from what is happening around them to get a clearer view of the bigger picture. When you are in the middle of everything it is hard to notice things like power struggles or people resisting change or avoiding work. Heifetz said that leaders who cannot move between the balcony and the ground level end up becoming "prisoners of the system" and they can not really lead their team through change anymore.
