Individual and Group Performance - 3 things I found interesting

1. The audience effect was interesting because I did not know that just having people around you can affect how you perform. Triplett found that children turned a small reel faster when playing in pairs than alone which is called social facilitation. However if the task is difficult being watched can actually make you worse because the arousal causes stress and you mess up more.

2. I did not expect social loafing to be a real thing because you would think people try harder in a group but they actually do less. Ringelmann did a study where people pulled a rope attached to a pressure gauge and the more people pulling the further below their individual potential they performed. Diffusion of responsibility explains this because people just feel less responsible when the team is bigger.

3. Schmitt did a study on how being watched changes things depending on what kind of task you are doing. Students typed their name faster when an experimenter was watching them. but when they had to type their name backwards with numbers between the letters they got slower when being watched. so it depends on whether the task is simple or not.
